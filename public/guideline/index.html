TBSP · Visual guideline · V1 · September 2026

Drafting paper, drafting ink, one amber marker.

How the TBSP website is built, and how the same system carries into the product. Values are taken from the shipped stylesheet. The markdown source of this guideline lives in the site repo at docs/BRAND-VISUAL-GUIDELINE-V1.md.

01 · Principles

Five rules cover most decisions.

Two grounds, one markerPaper and ink are the only surfaces. Amber is a fill for nodes, shadows, button grounds and highlighted phrases. It is not a text colour on paper.
Construction over decorationStructure comes from hairlines and bands. Cells share borders edge to edge. No gaps, no radius, no blur.
One decorative gestureA hard, zero-blur offset shadow in amber. On dark panels and the primary dark button, nowhere else.
Labels are condensedMetadata is IBM Plex Sans Condensed, uppercase, tracked. Monospace is for code and glyph icons only.
Derive, do not inventNew figures reuse the grammar in section 04. A new vocabulary reads as off-brand immediately.

04 · Construction grammar

The shapes that make something look like TBSP.

Everything on the site, including every illustration, is built from these. The test for a new figure: could the site's own CSS classes produce it?

Rule with punched nodes

A continuous 1px rule with 21px circular nodes sitting on it. Each node carries the ground colour to mask the line. The active node is amber with an ink digit. This is the signature.

05 · Product UI

The product is the dark panel, full screen.

The site already depicts the product as ink panels on paper. In the product those panels are the surface. Same tokens, tighter density, and one semantic change: amber means exactly one thing, a person must look at or decide this. Nothing automated is amber. Pending is neutral grey.

Carries over

  • Stage row as the pipeline header of every work item, same 21px nodes and condensed labels.
  • Topline on every panel, drawer and modal: what it is on the left, its state on the right.
  • Evidence cells for findings, requirements and decisions; the gate variant for anything awaiting a person.
  • Chips for sources and agents. Status marks with product semantics. One raised plate per view.
  • Density: 4px base, 36px dense rows, 44px two-line rows, 16 to 20px panel padding. Largest text 24px. Tabular numerals everywhere.
  • States: hover is a 4% wash; selected is the accent wash plus a 2px leading edge; focus is a 2px amber outline, always visible; live activity reuses the circuit pulse.

Does not carry over

  • Display type above 24px.
  • The offset shadow. No shadows in the product at all.
  • Amber fills larger than a button or a node, and any amber that means "pending" or "in progress".
  • The 112px rhythm and section numbering as decoration. Product numbers are counts, steps or IDs.
  • Radius anywhere except node and badge circles. Zebra striping; rows are divided by hairlines.
  • Icons with fills or emoji. Line icons at 16px, 1.5px stroke, square caps, colour following adjacent text.
06 · Do not

The short list.

  • No blue, purple, green or teal accent. Under Plex, blue reads as IBM Carbon and then as the Cognition and Warp cluster.
  • No warm cream, terracotta, rust or forest green. That is the previous system and the Anthropic and Cursor cluster.
  • No monospace labels. The sans plus designer-mono pairing is the dev-tool uniform.
  • No italic or serif for emphasis. Highlight instead.
  • No amber text on paper. No white text on amber.
  • No rounded corners, blurred shadows, gradients or glows.
  • No two dark bands touching, including the footer.
  • No diagrams with gaps between boxes or arrows drawn as artwork. Build from the grammar.
  • No device mockups, browser chrome, stock photography, people or robots.
  • No numbering of a grid of equals. Numbers mark real sequences.
